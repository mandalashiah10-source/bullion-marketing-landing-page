SEO & Local SEO

What is SEO and why does my Siliguri business need it?

SEO means making your website and online presence easier for Google to understand, so you appear when people search for what you offer. If customers in Siliguri search on Google for what you sell, SEO helps them find you instead of your competitors, without you paying for every click.

How can local SEO help my business attract nearby customers?

When people in Siliguri search for something “near me”, or look for a service in areas like Sevoke Road, Hill Cart Road, Matigara or Pradhan Nagar, Google shows local businesses first. Local SEO helps you appear there by improving your Google Business Profile, using the right local keywords and building genuine reviews. The result is more calls, more direction requests and more walk-in customers.

Does Bullion Marketing provide Google Business Profile management?

Yes, as part of our SEO and Local SEO service. For businesses in Siliguri we help set up or improve your profile with the right details, categories, photos and working hours, add regular updates, and guide you on collecting genuine customer reviews, so you show up better on Google Search and Google Maps.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a steady process. Most businesses begin to see movement in about three to six months, depending on the competition and where your website starts. It takes time, but the results last.

Can you guarantee the first position on Google?

No honest agency can guarantee a number one ranking, because Google decides the rankings and keeps changing them. What we can do is follow proven practices, improve your website and content, and track your progress every month.

What does your SEO work include?

Depending on your needs, it can include finding the keywords your customers use, improving page titles and descriptions, making your website faster and mobile friendly, creating helpful content, and strengthening your presence on Google Maps.

Do I need a blog for SEO?

A blog is not compulsory, but it helps. Answering your customers’ common questions in blog posts gives you more chances to appear on Google and builds trust. We can help you plan and create content that matches your business.

Performance Ads

Can Bullion Marketing manage Google Ads and Meta Ads for my business?

Yes. Our Performance Ads service covers setting up your campaigns, choosing the right audience and keywords, creating the ads, tracking enquiries and sales, and improving results over time. We can target people in Siliguri, Matigara, Pradhan Nagar, Sevoke Road and nearby areas, so your budget reaches customers who can actually visit or buy from you. Your ad budget is separate from our service fee and is spent directly on Google and Meta.

What is the difference between Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Google Ads shows your business to people who are actively searching for what you sell, so they are often ready to act. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) show your business to people based on their interests, location and behaviour, which is great for finding new customers and building awareness. Many businesses use both.

How much should I spend on ads?

There is no single answer, because it depends on your goal, your competition and your area. A local business in Siliguri often starts with a modest budget, learns what works and then increases it. We will suggest a starting budget during your consultation.

Do you charge a separate fee for managing ads?

Our service fee and your ad budget are separate. The ad budget is paid to Google or Meta for showing your ads, and our fee covers planning, setting up, managing and improving your campaigns.

How soon will I get enquiries from ads?

Ads can start bringing enquiries within a few days to a few weeks once they are approved and running. The first period is also for testing, so results usually improve as we learn which ads and audiences work best.

How do you track whether my ads are working?

We set up tracking for calls, form submissions and other actions wherever possible, and review the numbers regularly. In your monthly report you see how much was spent, how many enquiries you received and what we plan to improve.
